CORE SCIENTIFIC MESSAGE:
Spatially aligned camera, LiDAR and 4D radar observations still mix shared responses, modality-specific cues, and background responses. Cross-modal consistency alone does not tell us whether a response concerns an object: a road/background can also be consistently observed. Conversely, differences may carry useful sensing information. ObjDec learns shared and modality-specific representations in corresponding local BEV regions, with object-region training constraints, and uses their predicted object relevance to guide fusion. These representations affect fusion itself, not only an auxiliary training loss.
